About

Helping Heart Family Rwanda is a passionate organization dedicated to building communities where every child's rights are fiercely respected and equally provided. Through a comprehensive approach that integrates family support, legal aid, and education, they create protective environments for Rwanda's most vulnerable children.

Their programs span from providing legal assistance to families navigating child rights challenges, to offering educational scholarships and after-school support programs. By strengthening the family unit and ensuring access to justice and education, Helping Heart Family Rwanda creates lasting change from the ground up.
