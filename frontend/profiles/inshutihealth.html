Health Community Empowerment Nyagatare

About

Inshuti Health Organization is transforming lives in Nyagatare District through a powerful combination of health equity initiatives and economic empowerment programs. By recognizing that health and economic stability are deeply interconnected, they address both dimensions simultaneously for maximum community impact.

The organization runs community health education programs, facilitates access to healthcare services, and creates economic opportunities through savings groups and micro-enterprise development. Their integrated approach ensures that communities not only receive healthcare support but also build the economic resilience needed to sustain healthy lifestyles long-term.
