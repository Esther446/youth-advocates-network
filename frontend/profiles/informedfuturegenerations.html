About

Informed Future Generations is a youth-led organization operating in Rwanda's Eastern Province, dedicated to eradicating harmful social norms that hold communities back. Through their flagship 'Like Your Sister' program, they promote gender equality and challenge deeply ingrained stereotypes.

Their approach combines grassroots community engagement, peer education, and culturally sensitive dialogue to create lasting attitudinal shifts. By empowering young people to become agents of change, they are building a generation that values equality and respect.
