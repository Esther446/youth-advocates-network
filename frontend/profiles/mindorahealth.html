About

Mindora Health stands at the intersection of technology and mental health, leveraging artificial intelligence to deliver accessible, personalized wellbeing support to Rwanda's youth. In a region where mental health services are scarce and stigmatized, Mindora Health provides a discreet, always-available digital companion.

Their AI-driven platform offers guided mental health support, connecting users with resources, coping strategies, and professional referrals when needed. Combined with community youth programs and mental health awareness campaigns, Mindora Health is pioneering a tech-enabled approach to mental wellbeing that is culturally sensitive and deeply impactful.
