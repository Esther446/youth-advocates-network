About

The Nursing Research Club brings together passionate nursing professionals and students who are committed to advancing community health through rigorous research and evidence-based clinical practices. Their work bridges the gap between academic research and practical healthcare delivery in Rwanda.

Members conduct community-focused research projects, publish findings, and implement evidence-based interventions that directly improve patient outcomes. Through mentorship, journal clubs, and collaborative research initiatives, the club fosters a culture of inquiry and excellence in nursing practice that benefits communities across Rwanda.
