Health Equity Innovation

About

Oazis Health is a dynamic youth-led initiative committed to bridging healthcare gaps for marginalized communities across Rwanda. Through innovative grassroots approaches, the organization identifies and addresses health disparities that traditional systems often overlook.

By combining community health education, mobile outreach clinics, and partnerships with local health facilities, Oazis Health ensures that quality healthcare reaches those who need it most. Their model emphasizes sustainable, community-driven solutions that empower local populations to take charge of their own health outcomes.
