About

Rise and Live Organization is dedicated to transforming the lives of teen mothers across Rwanda. By providing mental health support, reproductive health education, and life skills training, they empower young mothers to overcome societal stigma and build fulfilling, independent lives.

The organization recognizes that teen mothers face unique challenges that require compassionate, holistic support. Through peer support groups, counseling services, and vocational training programs, Rise and Live creates safe spaces where young mothers can heal, learn, and grow into confident individuals who lead by example.
