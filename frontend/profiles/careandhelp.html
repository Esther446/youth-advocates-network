About

Care and Help Child Organization is dedicated to creating a safe and nurturing environment for children in Rwanda. Our mission is to ensure that every child has access to quality education, healthcare, and a supportive community.

We focus on mental well-being and empowering young minds to build brighter futures through community-led initiatives and individual mentorship programs.
