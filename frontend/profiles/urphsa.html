About

URPHSA (University of Rwanda Public Health Students' Association) has been bridging the gap between academic knowledge and real-world health impact since 2017. As a student-led organization, they empower the next generation of public health professionals to apply their learning in communities that need it most.

Through research initiatives, community health campaigns, and advocacy programs, URPHSA members gain hands-on experience while making tangible improvements in public health outcomes. Their work spans health education, disease prevention, and policy advocacy, creating a pipeline of skilled, socially-conscious health professionals.
