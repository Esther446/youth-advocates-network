HEZA INITIATIVE

Cultivating maternal and child health through nutrition, agriculture, and empowerment

Nutrition Health Youth Empowerment

About

Heza Initiative takes a holistic approach to maternal and child health, recognizing that nutrition, agriculture, and community empowerment are deeply interconnected. By addressing the root causes of malnutrition through sustainable farming practices and health education, they create lasting improvements in community well-being.

The initiative works directly with mothers and caregivers, providing them with the knowledge, resources, and support needed to ensure proper nutrition for their families. Their agricultural programs promote locally-adapted, nutrient-rich crops while their health outreach ensures communities understand the vital link between nutrition and child development.
