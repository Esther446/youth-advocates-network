About

Aspire Debate Rwanda has been at the forefront of youth empowerment through debate education since 2014. By equipping young people with critical thinking, public speaking, and analytical skills, the organization transforms how Rwanda's next generation engages with the world's most pressing issues.

Through structured debate programs, workshops, and competitions, Aspire Debate Rwanda nurtures confident leaders who can articulate their ideas, respect diverse perspectives, and drive positive change in their communities.
